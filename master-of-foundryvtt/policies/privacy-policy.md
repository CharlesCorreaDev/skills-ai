# Foundry VTT Privacy & Telemetry Guidelines

> Source: https://foundryvtt.com/article/privacy-policy/

## Data Privacy Essentials
1. **Self-Hosted Privacy:** Foundry VTT is self-hosted; game data, chat logs, and user credentials remain on the host server.
2. **Module Telemetry:** Community modules collecting telemetry or external data MUST obtain user consent and adhere to strict privacy practices.
