# Foundry VTT Trademark & Branding Guidelines

> Source: https://foundryvtt.com/article/branding/

## Branding Rules for Community Packages
1. **Naming:** Do not name your package starting with 'Foundry VTT' as if official; prefer 'MyModule for Foundry VTT'.
2. **Logos:** Use official community badge assets without altering core trademark logos.
3. **Endorsement:** Do not claim official endorsement or partnership with Foundry Gaming LLC without explicit written authorization.
