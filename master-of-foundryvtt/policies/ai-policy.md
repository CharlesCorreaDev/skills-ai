# Foundry VTT AI Generation & Tooling Policy

> Source: https://foundryvtt.com/article/ai-policy/

## Principles for AI Assistance & Content
1. **Respect for Creator Rights:** AI-generated assets, text, or code must not infringe upon third-party copyrights or trademarks.
2. **Transparency in Distribution:** Packages distributed on the Foundry package repository that heavily feature AI-generated content should disclose its nature.
3. **Safety & Stability:** AI coding assistants must adhere strictly to public APIs and safe deprecation lifecycles.
