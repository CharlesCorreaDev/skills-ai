# Licensing & Editions

- ### On this Page

  - [Trials](docs_5x__editions.md#trials)

Every Craft project starts with a free **Solo** license, which is valid for public and private use, indefinitely.

Additional features like user management are available by [upgrading](#upgrading) to a paid **Team** or **Pro** license. The current Craft edition and version are displayed at the bottom of every control panel screen.

Throughout the documentation, edition-specific features are called out with Team and Pro badges. If you don’t see a badge on a page or section, it describes features available to all Craft editions.

## [#](#upgrading)Upgrading

To try out a paid Craft edition, visit the **Plugin Store** in the [control panel](docs_5x__system__control-panel.md#plugin-store), and click **Upgrade Craft CMS** in the sidebar:

my-project.ddev.site/admin/plugin-store

![Screenshot of Craft’s in-app plugin store opened to the editions screen](/docs/assets/img/craft-license-in-app-upgrade.50b2b69c.png)

Selecting an edition will update [project config](docs_5x__system__project-config.md) and replace the **Try** button with a green **Installed as a trial** message.

## [#](#plugins)Plugins

Some plugins have editions, as well! The [Plugin Store  (opens new window)](https://plugins.craftcms.com/) displays editions in the sidebar, each having a **Try** button. Check the **Editions** tab for more information from the developer.

## [#](#trials)Trials

You are allowed to test paid Craft and plugin editions on private domains for as long as you need. However, deploying an unlicensed edition of Craft to a [public domain  (opens new window)](https://craftcms.com/knowledge-base/how-craft-license-enforcement-works#public-domains) will cause the control panel to display licensing issues to all [administrators](docs_5x__system__user-management.md#admin-accounts).
