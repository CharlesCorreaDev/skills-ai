# About Craft Commerce

Craft Commerce is the powerful and flexible first-party ecommerce platform for [Craft CMS  (opens new window)](https://craftcms.com).

- **Owners** appreciate Commerce’s easy-to-use administration interface, which is deeply integrated with Craft’s control panel.
- **Customers** love the hand-crafted shopping and checkout experiences that Commerce makes possible.
- **Developers** enjoy the freedom and speed with which they can deliver high-quality, outsize ecommerce solutions.

Familiarity with [Craft CMS  (opens new window)](https://craftcms.com/) is essential when embarking on a Craft Commerce project! Take a spin through the [Craft CMS documentation](https://craftcms.com/docs/5.x/) to get a sense for the blank-slate approach to content modeling
