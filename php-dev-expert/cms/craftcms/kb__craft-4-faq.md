# Craft 4 FAQ

### Will there be any additional licensing costs to upgrade to Craft 4?

No. From a licensing perspective, Craft 4 is like any other update: as long as your license is active, you’re entitled to updates.

### When will Craft 4 be released?

In [our Craft 4 beta announcement](https://craftcms.com/blog/craft-4-beta), we clarified that 4.0.0 is planned for May 4, 2022.

### How can I try out Craft 4 while it’s in beta?

See [Upgrading from Craft 3](https://craftcms.com/docs/4.x/upgrade.html) in the Craft 4 documentation. We’ve also updated our [demos](https://craftcms.com/demo) to include Craft 4 and Commerce 4 options.

### Can I do anything now that’ll make it easier to upgrade to Craft 4 later?

Yes, you can get your Craft 3 sites up to date and save your future self some time. See our [Preparing for Craft 4](kb__preparing-for-craft-4.md) guide.

### Who is the Craft 4 beta meant for?

If you’re reading this, probably you! We’re looking for feedback from anyone that has it: content authors, project developers, plugin developers, devops wizards, etc. If you need help seeing Craft 4 in action, please reach out to us at [support@​craftcms.​com](mailto:support@craftcms.com).

### How can I tell which of my site’s plugins are compatible with Craft 4?

In the Craft Plugin Store, each plugin lists its Craft version compatibility in its sidebar details. Look for **Compatibility**, which may list *Craft 3* and/​or *Craft 4*.

### What’s the best way to file issues for the beta?

Please file an issue [on GitHub](https://github.com/craftcms/cms/issues) like you normally would and use the ​“Craft 4 Beta” issue template.
