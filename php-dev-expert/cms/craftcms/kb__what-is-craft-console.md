# What is Craft Console?

![](https://craftcms.com/dist/images/icon-loading.png)

![Console Organization Members]()

[Craft Console](https://console.craftcms.com) is our centralized [Cloud](https://craftcms.com/cloud) project and license management platform. It also powers the Craft [Plugin Store](https://plugins.craftcms.com), and provides flexible tools for organizing and delegating access to your collaborators.

Accounts are free, and it only takes a moment to [register](https://console.craftcms.com).

![](https://craftcms.com/dist/images/icon-tip.svg)

Craft Console replaced Craft ID in December 2022. Any references you may find to Craft ID (in past official communication, documentation, or other community resources) refer to features that have been rolled into the new platform.

## Accounts + Organizations [#](#accounts-organizations "Direct link to Accounts + Organizations")

Your Craft Console account represents you as an individual—but everyone can create [organizations](kb__craft-console-organizations.md) and invite the rest of their team. This is a great way for [clients](kb__craft-console-for-clients.md) to share licenses, grant [purchasing power](kb__craft-console-for-clients.md#approving-orders), or set up access to [Cloud projects](#craft-cloud) for developers.

## License Management [#](#license-management "Direct link to License Management")

Craft and plugin licenses can be purchased as an individual or through an [organization](kb__craft-console-organizations.md#making-a-purchase). Those licenses are automatically tied to that account context—but you can always transfer them later, when a project changes hands or your team grows.

![](https://craftcms.com/dist/images/icon-tip.svg)

Legacy licenses (Craft 2 and earlier) will automatically be associated with your account upon registration!

## Craft Cloud [#](#craft-cloud "Direct link to Craft Cloud")

You can spin up a new project on our first-party hosting platform [Craft Cloud](https://craftcms.com/cloud) from any personal account or organization with a payment method on file.

![](https://craftcms.com/dist/images/icon-loading.png)

![Console Tour: Craft Cloud]()

## Developer Tools [#](#developer-tools "Direct link to Developer Tools")

Craft Console is also where you’ll manage individual [developer support plans](https://craftcms.com/support-services) and your organization’s [Craft Partner network](https://craftcms.com/become-a-partner) profile—as well as any plugins you’ve [submitted](https://craftcms.com/docs/5.x/extend/plugin-store.html) to the Plugin Store.
