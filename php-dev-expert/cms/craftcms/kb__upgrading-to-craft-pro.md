# Upgrading to Craft Pro

Craft’s Pro edition offers [several benefits](https://craftcms.com/pricing) over the Solo edition, including unlimited user accounts, enhanced content previewing, and more.

It costs $399 (USD) per Craft installation, and a single license can be run across multiple development, staging, and production environments.

To check which edition you’re currently running, look for the edition badge in the control panel footer:

![](https://craftcms.com/dist/images/icon-loading.png)

![Craft Solo badge]()

Click the “Upgrade to Craft Pro” link. That will take you to the **Plugin Store** → **Upgrade Craft CMS** page, which will list the two editions, and a “Buy now” button under “Pro”.

If Craft detects that it’s running in a development environment, you’ll also have the option to [try Craft Pro for free](kb__try-craft-pro-plugins-before-buying.md).

![](https://craftcms.com/dist/images/icon-loading.png)

![Upgrade in app pricing view]()

Craft Solo, Team, and Pro feature comparison, viewed from the control panel’s plugin store.

Once you’ve upgraded, you will immediately be able to start taking advantage of the Pro-exclusive features.

Applies to
Craft CMS 4 and Craft CMS 3.
