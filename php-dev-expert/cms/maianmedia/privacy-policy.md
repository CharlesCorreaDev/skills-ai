### Privacy Policy

This policy covers how Maian Media use your personal information. Maian Media take your privacy seriously and will take all measures to protect your personal information. Maian Media tries to fully comply
with the [GDPR](https://ico.org.uk/) law that came into affect on May 25th 2018.
> Address / Contact Information

Any address or contact information received will only be used to fulfill your order and is used solely for internal record/book keeping. This enables you to obtain
receipts for your purchases and Maian Media to keep book keeping, which are required by law.
> Email Address

Your email is used to let you know of related products, product coupons and product updates related to your purchased product if you are subscribed to our newsletter. Maian Media will never sell or redistribute your information to third parties or promotional companies. If you DO NOT subscribe, you will receive no after sales emails. You
can unsubscribe at any time via a promotional email unsubcribe link.
> IP Address

IP addresses are logged/collected as part of a business critical need to prevent fraud. Only the IP is logged which contains no user identifying information.
> External Website Links

My website may contain links to other websites of interest. However, once you have used these links to leave my site, you should note that Maian Media do not have any control over that other website. Therefore, Maian Media cannot be responsible for the protection and privacy of any information which you provide whilst visiting such sites and such sites are not governed by this privacy statement. You should exercise caution and look at the privacy statement applicable to the website in question.
> Cookies

TheMaian Media account system uses a session cookie that gets cleared when you close your browser. No other cookies are set.
> Analytics

This website does not use analytics tracking of any kind.
> SSL

This website is protected by a secure socket layer to encrypt data before it is transmitted across the web. Look for the padlock in your address bar for more info. Any data you send on the Maian Media website or product websites is encrypted during transmission. This website is hosted on secure servers in the United States & Amsterdam.
> [GDPR](https://ico.org.uk/) - Account Data/Deletion

If you are in the European Union you can at any time request to have your account and all related data deleted from the Maian Media database. You can also request
to see what information is stored relating to your account.  
  
Any questions, please [contact us](support.md).
