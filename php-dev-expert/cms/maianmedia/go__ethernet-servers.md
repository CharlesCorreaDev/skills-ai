## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long has Ethernet Servers been in business?

Ethernet Servers has been a privately owned, UK-registered limited company for over 11 years, since July 2014.

We are 100% self-funded, with no outside investors to please, parent companies, or other stakeholders.

### What exactly is Ethernet Servers all about?

We're the people who make sure websites, game servers, apps, VPNs and much more **get online**, and **stay online**.

If you've built a website using an application on your computer, you need somewhere to upload it to be accessible online. Or, likewise, if you've been running a Minecraft game server for yourself, but want to release it to be accessible 24/7 and to the world, you need somewhere to host it.

These examples are just two of many possible reasons you might need hosting.

### What payment methods do you accept?

We accept PayPal, Credit/Debit Card, Bank Transfer, Alipay, as well as cryptocurrency.

### Who is your service suitable for?

Everyone who needs or wants an online presence!

We have services that are beginner-friendly, such as [Shared Hosting](https://www.ethernetservers.com/shared-hosting) plans which include features such as a website builder (no coding knowledge needed).

On the flip side, we have [KVM VPS](https://www.ethernetservers.com/vps-hosting) and  [Dedicated Servers](https://www.ethernetservers.com/dedicated-servers) for those who demand maximum control over the server environment.

Additionally, all services include 24/7 UK-based customer service - so you're never alone.
