### Frequently Asked Questions

A few questions we regularly get asked are shown below.
> Does your commercial licence work with sub domains as well as the main domain?

Yes, at no extra cost. Please see [this](sub-domain-licences.md) guide.
> Does your software work with PHPXX?

Please check the software website and the system requirements for supported versions.
> How do I generate a licence for your software?

Please log into your [account](accounts__view__dashboard.md) and follow the instructions.
> Can I remove the copyright notices in the software?

Yes, please see the relevant software website.
> Can I resell or rebrand the software?

Yes, if a white label licence is available. Check the relevant software website.
> Can you recommend a good hosting company for your software?

There are many good hosts and a few that offer the required services can be found [here](hosting.md).
> Do you offer software installation services?

Yes, please [contact us](support.md) for assistance.
> Can you keep me informed of new software releases?

Please subscribe to our [RSS Feed](rss.md) if you wish to be kept informed of general news or updates OR subscribe to our [newsletter](newsletter.md).
> Are you interested in partnerships and/or promoting your software?

Yes, please [contact us](support.md) if you have something in mind.
