# Getting Started

This section introduces the essentials of Joomla and guides new users through the first steps of setting up and managing a site. It covers the basics of what Joomla is, its core features, how to get it running, and the fundamental tasks you’ll perform when creating and organizing content.

This section is designed to give you a smooth start, from installation to adding your first articles, menus, and modules, so you can quickly become familiar with Joomla’s structure and workflow.

## Introduction to Joomla

Understand what Joomla is and what it has to offer.

- [Introduction to Joomla](guide__user-guide__getting-started__getting-started-introduction-to-joomla.md) – A broad overview of Joomla as a content management system (CMS). This page explains its purpose, the types of websites you can create with it, and why Joomla is a flexible solution for beginners and advanced users alike.
- [Core Features](guide__user-guide__getting-started__getting-started-joomla-core-features.md) – Explore Joomla’s built-in functionality, including user management, menu and content handling, multilingual support, extensions, and template customization. This helps you understand the main tools at your disposal before you start building.

## Setup and Installation

Prepare your hosting and install Joomla.

- [Hosting Setup](guide__user-guide__getting-started__getting-started-hosting-setup.md) – Learn the technical requirements for running Joomla, such as PHP versions, database compatibility, and web server settings. This ensures your hosting environment is ready for a smooth installation.  
  [Installing Joomla](guide__user-guide__getting-started__getting-started-installing-joomla.md) – Step-by-step guidance on installing Joomla on your server. It walks you through the installation wizard and the key configuration details needed to get your site running.
- [Logging in to Joomla](guide__user-guide__getting-started__getting-started-logging-in-to-joomla.md) – Learn how to access the Joomla administrator dashboard after installation, where all site management tasks are performed.

## Creating Content

Start building and organizing your site content.

- [Articles and Categories](guide__user-guide__getting-started__getting-started-articles-and-categories.md) – Understand Joomla’s content structure, where articles are used for content, and categories group them together for better organization.
- [Adding a Category](guide__user-guide__getting-started__getting-started-adding-a-category.md) – Learn how to create categories to organize related articles, helping you manage content more effectively.
- [Adding an Article](guide__user-guide__getting-started__getting-started-adding-an-article.md) – Step-by-step instructions on writing, formatting, and publishing your first piece of content in Joomla.
- [Adding a Menu Item](guide__user-guide__getting-started__getting-started-adding-a-menu-item.md) – See how to link articles or categories to menus so visitors can easily navigate your site.
- [Adding a Module](guide__user-guide__getting-started__getting-started-adding-a-module.md) – Learn how to place dynamic blocks of content (like login forms, latest articles, or banners) around your site to enhance functionality and layout.

## Productivity Tools

Work more efficiently inside Joomla.

- [Keyboard Shortcuts](guide__user-guide__getting-started__getting-started-keyboard-shortcuts.md) – Save time by using built-in keyboard shortcuts for common actions in the Joomla administrator area, improving your workflow.
