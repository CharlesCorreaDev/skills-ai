# Managing Featured Products

From osCommerce Wiki

[Jump to navigation](#mw-head)
[Jump to search](#searchInput)

This manual explains how to manage featured products in your website’s admin area.

Start with the short video to get familiar.

The manual below provides step-by-step instructions.

#### **1) Access the Featured Products Section**

1. Log in to the **Admin Area** of your website.
2. Navigate to **My Marketing Tools → Featured Products**.

#### **2) Search and Filter Featured Products**

1. Use the search and filter options to find featured products by:
   - Product ID
   - Product model
   - Product name
   - UPC, EAN, ISBN
   - Date range
   - Featured type
   - Active or inactive status
   - Start and end dates

#### **3) Edit or Delete an Existing Featured Product**

1. Click on a featured product from the list.
2. Click **Edit**, update the required fields and click **Save**.
3. To **delete**, click the **Delete** button.

[![Image 942.png](/wiki/images/thumb/3/30/Image_942.png/799px-Image_942.png)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Image_942.png)

**4) Add a New Featured Product**

1. Click **Insert**.

[![Insert Product.jpg](/wiki/images/thumb/3/3f/Insert_Product.jpg/800px-Insert_Product.jpg)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Insert_Product.jpg)

2. Use the product search field to locate the product you want to feature.

3. Select the product from the dropdown list.

[![Select Product.jpg](/wiki/images/thumb/7/79/Select_Product.jpg/801px-Select_Product.jpg)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Select_Product.jpg)

4. Set the **featured status** (active/inactive).

5. (Optional) Set the **Start** and **End** dates to schedule when the product should be featured.

6. Click **Save**.

[![Image 943.png](/wiki/images/thumb/5/57/Image_943.png/799px-Image_943.png)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Image_943.png)

**5) Display on the Front End**

- Once configured, featured products will appear on the front end under the **Featured Products** section.

[![Image 944.png](/wiki/images/thumb/2/20/Image_944.png/799px-Image_944.png)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Image_944.png)
