# Managing Sales Statistics and Purchase Report

From osCommerce Wiki

[Jump to navigation](#mw-head)
[Jump to search](#searchInput)

This manual explains how to view, compare, and export sales statistics and purchase reports.

#### **Steps to Access and Use Sales Statistics**

1. **Access the Sales Statistics Section** Go to **Reports → Sales statistics** in the admin area of your website.
2. **Choose Precision and Comparison Options**
   - Use the dropdown lists to select **precision options** (for example, daily, weekly, or monthly reporting).
   - Use the **calendar selectors** to define the time period.
   - If needed, select an additional **month and year** to compare.
   - Check or uncheck the box for **Product details** to include or exclude product-level data.
3. **Select Order and Chart Options**
   - Use the dropdown lists to choose **order-related filters** (such as completed orders only).
   - Check the boxes next to the charts you want to display, for example:
     - Orders
     - Tax
     - Sub-Total / Total
     - Shipping
     - Amount Paid / Amount Due
4. **Apply or Reset Filters**
   - Click **Update** to refresh the data displayed.
   - Click **Reset** to clear currently selected filters.
5. **Save a Filter Preset (Optional)**
   - Enter a name for the current filter configuration (for example, test).
   - Click the **Save** button.
   - The saved filter will then be available from the **Sales statistics** dropdown list for future use.
6. **Export Sales Statistics**
   - Choose the export format: **CSV** or **XLS** by selecting the corresponding radio button.
   - Click the **Export** button to download the report.

[![](/wiki/images/thumb/9/9d/Sales_Stat_filter.jpg/801px-Sales_Stat_filter.jpg)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Sales_Stat_filter.jpg)

**Viewing Charts**

7. **Scroll Down to View Charts**

Charts will display automatically based on the selected options.

The common chart categories include:

- **Sales Daily Statistics**
- **Orders**
- **Tax**
- **Sub-Total and Total**
- **Shipping**
- **Amount Paid, and Amount Due**
