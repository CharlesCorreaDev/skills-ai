# Assigning Theme to Sales Channel and Deleting Sales Channel

From osCommerce Wiki

[Jump to navigation](#mw-head)
[Jump to search](#searchInput)

This manual explains how to assign a theme to a sales channel and how to delete unnecessary sales channels.

Watch the video for an overview.

**The manual below explains everything step by step.**

**1) Access the Admin Area**

1. Click on the **Admin** option after installing osCommerce.

[![Image 845.png](/wiki/images/thumb/c/c4/Image_845.png/800px-Image_845.png)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Image_845.png)

2. In the admin area, click on the **Sales channels** tab.

3. Under the **Web** tab, review the list of available sales channels.

**2) Assign a Theme to a Sales Channel**

1. Click on the **required sales channel**.
2. Click on the **Choose theme** button.

[![Image 844.png](/wiki/images/thumb/e/e7/Image_844.png/800px-Image_844.png)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Image_844.png)

3. Click on the **Choose theme** button.

[![Image 843.png](/wiki/images/thumb/7/71/Image_843.png/800px-Image_843.png)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Image_843.png)

4. Browse through the available themes and **click Assign** on the theme you want to apply (e.g., *Printshop*).

[![Screenshot 10.png](/wiki/images/thumb/c/cf/Screenshot_10.png/800px-Screenshot_10.png)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Screenshot_10.png)

5. Assign the necessary **banners**.

6. Click **Save**.

[![Image 841.png](/wiki/images/thumb/6/6a/Image_841.png/800px-Image_841.png)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Image_841.png)

The selected theme is now applied to the front end of the chosen sales channel.

[![Image 846.png](/wiki/images/thumb/e/e8/Image_846.png/800px-Image_846.png)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Image_846.png)

**3) Delete Unnecessary Sales Channels**

**Note:** The **default** sales channel (e.g., *demomode12*) **cannot** be deleted.

1. Go to the **Sales channels** tab.
2. Select the **sales channel** you want to delete.
3. Click **Delete**.

**4) Before Deleting a Sales Channel – Important Step**

1. Make sure that **required categories and products** are assigned to the **default** sales channel.

[![Image 1220.png](/wiki/images/thumb/f/f6/Image_1220.png/800px-Image_1220.png)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Image_1220.png)

2. Click on the **Assign categories/products** button.

3. **Check the boxes** next to the categories/products you want to assign.

4. Click on the **Assign** button.

[![Image 1219.png](/wiki/images/thumb/8/85/Image_1219.png/800px-Image_1219.png)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Image_1219.png)

**5) Remove Installation Directory for Security**

1. Click on the **Dashboard** tab.

2. If you see a security warning instructing you to remove the **Installation** directory, proceed with the next steps.

[![Image 1221.png](/wiki/images/thumb/f/f7/Image_1221.png/800px-Image_1221.png)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Image_1221.png)

3. Connect to your server via your preferred **FTP client**.

4. Remove the **Installation** directory.

5. Also remove **symbolic links** for any deleted sales channels.

[![Image 1222.png](/wiki/images/thumb/8/81/Image_1222.png/800px-Image_1222.png)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Image_1222.png)

6. Refresh the page to confirm the warning no longer appears.

[![Image 1223.png](/wiki/images/thumb/4/44/Image_1223.png/800px-Image_1223.png)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Image_1223.png)

You have now successfully assigned the required theme and cleaned up unnecessary sales channels. You may proceed with further website configuration.
