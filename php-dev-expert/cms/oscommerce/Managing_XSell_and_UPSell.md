# Managing XSell and UPSell

From osCommerce Wiki

[Jump to navigation](#mw-head)
[Jump to search](#searchInput)

This manual explains how to configure XSell and Upsell product recommendations.

Begin with the video to get an introduction.

The manual below explains everything step by step.

#### **1) Understanding XSell and Upsell**

- **XSell (Cross-Sell):** Suggests **related** or **complementary** products (e.g., accessories).
- **Upsell:** Suggests **higher-value** or **enhanced** product alternatives.

Both functionalities help customers discover additional items they may wish to purchase.

#### **2) Accessing Product Marketing Settings**

1. Go to the **admin area** of your website.
2. Click **Products/Catalogue → Products (Brands/Categories)**.
3. Select the required product.
4. Click **Edit** to open the product settings.
5. Navigate to the **Marketing** tab.

[![](/wiki/images/thumb/5/52/Prod_edit.jpg/800px-Prod_edit.jpg)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Prod_edit.jpg)

#### **3) Assigning Products to XSell or Upsell**

1. Under the Marketing tab, choose either:
   - **XSell** **Products** tab
   - **Upsell** **Products** tab
2. Search or select the product you want to assign.
3. Click **Add** to link the product.
4. The product will appear in the **Assigned Products** list.
5. Repeat as needed to assign multiple recommendations.

[![](/wiki/images/thumb/b/b2/Add_Prod.jpg/800px-Add_Prod.jpg)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Add_Prod.jpg)

#### **4) How Recommendations Display to Customers**

- When a customer views or purchases the configured product, they will see the **recommended XSell or Upsell products** displayed in the product view or checkout process.

[![](/wiki/images/thumb/3/37/Recommended_prod.jpg/558px-Recommended_prod.jpg)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Recommended_prod.jpg)

- Customers can click the recommended item’s **image or name** to open the product page and purchase it as usual.
