# Managing Products

From osCommerce Wiki

[Jump to navigation](#mw-head)
[Jump to search](#searchInput)

This user guide explains how to search for, manage, and edit products in your store.

Want to see how it works? Start with the video!

**The manual below explains everything step by step.**

#### **1) Access the Products Section**

1. Log in to the **admin area** of your website.
2. Navigate to **Products/Catalogue → Products (Brands/Categories)**.

#### **2) Search and Filter Products**

1. Use the **search field** to look for products by:
   - Product name, product description, category, category description, product page title, header description, keywords.
   - Identifiers such as **SKU, EAN, ASIN, ISBN, UPC**, product image name, or SEO page name.
2. Use **Filters** to refine results by:
   - Sales channel, brand, status, stock availability, low stock flag, featured flag,
   - Gift wrap option, virtual products, bundles, sale items, supplier, price, and weight.

[![Products search 1 1021.png](/wiki/images/thumb/1/10/Products_search_1_1021.png/801px-Products_search_1_1021.png)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Products_search_1_1021.png)

[![Products search 2 1022.png](/wiki/images/thumb/f/f6/Products_search_2_1022.png/800px-Products_search_2_1022.png)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Products_search_2_1022.png)

#### **3) Manage the Product List**

1. Use **drag and drop** to reorder products.
2. Use the **toggle** to switch products **on** or **off** (active/inactive).

[![Products drop toggle 1023.png](/wiki/images/thumb/0/06/Products_drop_toggle_1023.png/800px-Products_drop_toggle_1023.png)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Products_drop_toggle_1023.png)

3. Click **Add** **product** to create a new product, or **Edit** to modify an existing one.

[![Products add edit 1024.png](/wiki/images/thumb/3/39/Products_add_edit_1024.png/800px-Products_add_edit_1024.png)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Products_add_edit_1024.png)

#### **4) Assign Products to Sales Channels and Customer Groups**

1. Open the product and go to the **Assign/Restrict** tab.
2. Enable sale channels where the product should appear.
3. Assign the product to specific **customer groups** using the **Assign** switch.
4. To make the product unavailable for a group, **disable** its availability toggle.

**See [Assigning Products and Categories to Front Ends](Assigning_Products_and_Categories_to_Front_Ends.md "Assigning Products and Categories to Front Ends")**

[![Orders 5.jpg](/wiki/images/thumb/3/33/Orders_5.jpg/800px-Orders_5.jpg)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Orders_5.jpg)

#### **5) Configure Price and Cost**

1. Go to the **Price and Cost** tab.
2. Choose a **tax class** and define **net** or **gross** price.
3. (Optional) Base product price on **supplier cost** and allow auto-updates.
4. Set **gift wrap** and **shipping surcharges** if needed.
5. Configure **quantity-based discounts**, disable promotions if required, and set **RRP**.

[![Products Price Cost 1 1026.png](/wiki/images/thumb/f/f1/Products_Price_Cost_1_1026.png/801px-Products_Price_Cost_1_1026.png)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Products_Price_Cost_1_1026.png)

6. Under **Suppliers (cost)**:

- Add/edit supplier, set net cost and currency.
- Configure shop discount, margin, and surcharge.

[![Products Suppl sec 1027.png](/wiki/images/thumb/2/21/Products_Suppl_sec_1027.png/800px-Products_Suppl_sec_1027.png)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Products_Suppl_sec_1027.png)

**6) Enter Product Name and Description**

1. Go to the **Name and Description** tab.
2. Enter product name, full description, short description, and keywords.
3. Add multilingual content if necessary.
4. Enable **Show on all sales channels** if applicable.

[![Products Name descr 1 1028.png](/wiki/images/thumb/7/75/Products_Name_descr_1_1028.png/800px-Products_Name_descr_1_1028.png)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Products_Name_descr_1_1028.png)

[![Products Name descr 2 1029.png](/wiki/images/thumb/2/25/Products_Name_descr_2_1029.png/800px-Products_Name_descr_2_1029.png)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Products_Name_descr_2_1029.png)

#### **7) Set Main Product Details**

1. Go to the **Main Details** tab.
2. Set product **status** (active/inactive) and **manual status control** if needed.
3. Configure:
   - Unlimited stock flag.
   - Demo product flag.
   - Product identifiers (SKU, EAN, ASIN, UPC).

[![Products 10.jpg](/wiki/images/thumb/6/6c/Products_10.jpg/799px-Products_10.jpg)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Products_10.jpg)

4. Select or create a **Brand**.

5. Set:

- Unit label,
- Featured status,
- Google Shopping product type,
- Customization template.

6. Set **Availability date** and **New until** date.

[![Main det 11.jpg](/wiki/images/thumb/4/4f/Main_det_11.jpg/800px-Main_det_11.jpg)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Main_det_11.jpg)

#### **8) Manage Stock and Availability**

1. Go to the **Stock** section.
2. Choose stock mode: real stock, always in stock, out of stock, hidden, pre-order, etc.
3. Enable or disable the **Add to cart** button.
4. Set **delivery terms**, **out-of-stock level**, and **out-of-stock actions**.
5. (Optional) Enable **Stock splitting**.
6. For stock history, refer to stock management documentation. *See [Managing Stock](Managing_Stock.md "Managing Stock")*.

[![Products Main detail 3 1032.png](/wiki/images/thumb/c/ca/Products_Main_detail_3_1032.png/800px-Products_Main_detail_3_1032.png)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Products_Main_detail_3_1032.png)

#### **9) Assign Attributes and Manage Inventory**

1. Go to **Attributes and Inventory** tab.
2. Select attribute values and click **>>** to assign them.
3. The attribute value(s) will appear in the **Assigned attributes** section.

[![Products Attrib1 1044.png](/wiki/images/thumb/0/0f/Products_Attrib1_1044.png/800px-Products_Attrib1_1044.png)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Products_Attrib1_1044.png)

4. Click **Set up details** to adjust how attributes affect **price** and **weight**.

[![Products Attrib2 1045.png](/wiki/images/thumb/3/36/Products_Attrib2_1045.png/800px-Products_Attrib2_1045.png)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Products_Attrib2_1045.png)

5. For **virtual products**:

- Upload/downloadable file,
- Set expiry days and download limits.

[![Products Attrib3 1046.png](/wiki/images/thumb/0/09/Products_Attrib3_1046.png/800px-Products_Attrib3_1046.png)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Products_Attrib3_1046.png)

**10) Upload and Manage Images**

1. Open the **Images** tab.
2. Upload single or multiple images.
3. Set **SEO title**, **ALT text**, and link images to video if needed.
4. Optionally:
   - Use external image URLs,
   - Exclude from watermarking,
   - Rearrange images via drag and drop.
   - Upload product image map if any.
   - Set image properties in different languages.

[![Products Images1 1033.png](/wiki/images/thumb/a/a7/Products_Images1_1033.png/800px-Products_Images1_1033.png)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Products_Images1_1033.png)

[![Products Images2 1034.png](/wiki/images/thumb/0/0c/Products_Images2_1034.png/800px-Products_Images2_1034.png)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Products_Images2_1034.png)

#### **11) Add Product Videos**

1. Go to the **Video** tab.
2. Upload a video or paste a **YouTube link**.

[![Products Video 1035.png](/wiki/images/thumb/7/7a/Products_Video_1035.png/800px-Products_Video_1035.png)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Products_Video_1035.png)

#### **12) Set Size and Packaging**

1. Open **Size and Packaging** tab.
2. Mark product as **virtual** if applicable.
3. Set **weight**, and configure **volume** or use 3 available options to calculate bundle volume.

[![Products Size Packaging 1036.png](/wiki/images/thumb/7/73/Products_Size_Packaging_1036.png/800px-Products_Size_Packaging_1036.png)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Products_Size_Packaging_1036.png)

#### **13) Configure SEO Settings**

1. Go to the **SEO** tab.
2. Set or override:
   - SEO page name (URL),
   - Noindex/Nofollow flags,
   - Canonical URL,
   - Page title, description, meta data,

**See also [Managing SEO](Managing_SEO.md "Managing SEO")**

[![Products SEO1 1037.png](/wiki/images/thumb/c/cf/Products_SEO1_1037.png/800px-Products_SEO1_1037.png)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Products_SEO1_1037.png)

- H1, H2, H3 tags,
- ALT/title templates for images.

[![Products SEO2 1038.png](/wiki/images/thumb/d/df/Products_SEO2_1038.png/800px-Products_SEO2_1038.png)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Products_SEO2_1038.png)

#### **14) Manage Marketing Features**

1. Go to the **Marketing** tab.
2. Link **Upsell** and **Xsell** products.

**See** [Managing XSell and UPSell](Managing_XSell_and_UPSell.md "Managing XSell and UPSell")

[![Products Mark1 1039.png](/wiki/images/thumb/3/35/Products_Mark1_1039.png/800px-Products_Mark1_1039.png)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Products_Mark1_1039.png)

3. Manage **Giveaways**.

[![Products Mark2 1040.png](/wiki/images/thumb/5/5e/Products_Mark2_1040.png/800px-Products_Mark2_1040.png)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Products_Mark2_1040.png)

4. Track product **popularity statistics**.

[![Products Mark3 1041.png](/wiki/images/thumb/2/2f/Products_Mark3_1041.png/800px-Products_Mark3_1041.png)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Products_Mark3_1041.png)

#### **15) Assign Product Properties**

1. Go to the **Properties** tab.
2. Set technical product properties and values.

**See [Managing Filters on Categories](Managing_Filters_on_Categories.md "Managing Filters on Categories")**

[![Products Prop 1042.png](/wiki/images/thumb/a/a8/Products_Prop_1042.png/800px-Products_Prop_1042.png)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Products_Prop_1042.png)

#### **16) Add Internal Notes**

1. Open the **Notes** tab.
2. Create internal notes for team use.

[![Products Notes 1043.png](/wiki/images/thumb/5/54/Products_Notes_1043.png/800px-Products_Notes_1043.png)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Products_Notes_1043.png)
