# Managing Brands

From osCommerce Wiki

[Jump to navigation](#mw-head)
[Jump to search](#searchInput)

This manual explains how to create, edit, and manage brands in your website’s admin area.

Start with the short video to get familiar.

**The manual below explains everything step by step.**

#### **1) Access the Brands Section**

1. Log in to the **admin area** of your website.
2. Navigate to **Products/Catalogue → Products (Brands/Categories)**.
3. Click the **Brands** tab.

#### **2) View and Reorder Brands**

1. Review the list of existing brands.
2. Use **drag and drop** to reorder brands as required.

[![Brands drop1013.png](/wiki/images/thumb/1/11/Brands_drop1013.png/800px-Brands_drop1013.png)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Brands_drop1013.png)

#### **3) Add a New Brand**

1. Click the **Add brand** or **Create brand** button.
2. Proceed to configure the brand using the tabs described below.

[![Brands add new1014.png](/wiki/images/thumb/2/20/Brands_add_new1014.png/802px-Brands_add_new1014.png)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Brands_add_new1014.png)

**4) Edit or Delete a Brand**

1. To **edit** a brand, click the **pencil** icon next to it.
2. To **delete** a brand, click the **bin** icon next to it.

[![Brands pencil trash can1015.png](/wiki/images/thumb/8/85/Brands_pencil_trash_can1015.png/800px-Brands_pencil_trash_can1015.png)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Brands_pencil_trash_can1015.png)

#### **5) Configure Main Details**

1. Open the **Main details** tab while adding/editing a brand.
2. Set the **Brand name**.
3. Specify **Out of stock level** limit (if needed).
4. Upload or set **Gallery image** and **Hero image**.

[![Brands Main det tab1016.png](/wiki/images/thumb/a/ab/Brands_Main_det_tab1016.png/801px-Brands_Main_det_tab1016.png)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Brands_Main_det_tab1016.png)

#### **6) Name and Description**

1. Open the **Name and description** tab.
2. Enter the **Brand description**.
3. Assign links to: **Pages**, **Products**, **Categories**, **Delivery Location**, **Brands**, and **Common links**.
4. Enter **search keywords** for the brand.

[![Brands Name descr tab1017.png](/wiki/images/thumb/0/00/Brands_Name_descr_tab1017.png/801px-Brands_Name_descr_tab1017.png)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Brands_Name_descr_tab1017.png)

**7) SEO Settings**

1. Open the **SEO** tab.
2. Set or override the **Manufacturers URL**.
3. Enter **Brand SEO name**, **Meta description**, and **Title meta-tag**.
4. Configure **H1 / H2 / H3** tags (add multiple H2/H3 if required).

[![Brands SEO tab1018.png](/wiki/images/thumb/0/08/Brands_SEO_tab1018.png/801px-Brands_SEO_tab1018.png)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Brands_SEO_tab1018.png)

#### **8) Suppliers**

1. Open the **Suppliers** tab.
2. Choose a **default supplier** for this brand (if applicable).
3. Use the **Select /** **Add new supplier** or **Edit** buttons to manage supplier entries.

[![Brands Suppliers1 tab1019.png](/wiki/images/thumb/6/62/Brands_Suppliers1_tab1019.png/800px-Brands_Suppliers1_tab1019.png)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Brands_Suppliers1_tab1019.png)

[![Brands Suppliers2 tab1020.png](/wiki/images/thumb/9/92/Brands_Suppliers2_tab1020.png/800px-Brands_Suppliers2_tab1020.png)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Brands_Suppliers2_tab1020.png)

#### **9) Filters**

1. Open the **Filters** tab.
2. Activate product filters so customers can sort by **keywords**, **price ranges**, and **categories**.
3. Click **View Values** to review active filter values (price ranges, and categories assigned.).

[![Filters tab.jpg](/wiki/images/thumb/2/2a/Filters_tab.jpg/800px-Filters_tab.jpg)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Filters_tab.jpg)

#### **10) Final Confirmation**

1. Review all brand settings.
2. Click **Save** to apply changes.

With brands configured, it is easier to organize, filter, and present product ranges to customers.
