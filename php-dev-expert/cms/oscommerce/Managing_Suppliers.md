# Managing Suppliers

From osCommerce Wiki

[Jump to navigation](#mw-head)
[Jump to search](#searchInput)

This manual explains how to add, edit, and manage supplier details in your website’s admin area.

Watch the video for a brief overview.

Use the manual to explore the functionality in detail.

**1) Accessing Supplier Settings**

1. Access the **admin area** of your website.
2. Navigate to **Products/Catalogue → Suppliers** tabs.

**2) Managing Suppliers**

Under this tab, you can:

- **Add** a new supplier.
- **Edit** existing supplier details.
- **Delete** a supplier (if it is not set as the default).
- **Enable or disable** supplier status (if it is not the default supplier).

[![Image 930.png](/wiki/images/thumb/d/de/Image_930.png/799px-Image_930.png)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Image_930.png)

**3) Adding or Editing Supplier Details**

When adding or editing a supplier, you can configure the following information:

- **Supplier name**
- **Default delivery time (days)** — from / to
- **Default tax rate (%)**
- **Price with tax** flag — yes / no
- **Additional fields**: AWRS, Sage Code, Payment delay (days), Supply delay (days), Condition, and Condition description
- **Company details**: Name, Tax/VAT ID, Contact name, Contact phone(s), Address
- **Associated currencies** — one or several
- **Authentication data** — Email and password

[![Image 931.png](/wiki/images/thumb/2/26/Image_931.png/799px-Image_931.png)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Image_931.png)

**4) Setting Quantity Discounts and Price Formulas**

For existing suppliers, you can define:

- **Quantity discounts (%)**
- **Default price formulas**, based on:
  - Currency
  - Cost range (from X to Y or “cost not less than”)
  - Cost, discount, surcharge, and margin values

You can also configure **discount**, **surcharge**, and **margin** settings individually.

[![Image 932.png](/wiki/images/thumb/c/c5/Image_932.png/799px-Image_932.png)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Image_932.png)

  **5) Final Step**

After updating supplier information, **save your changes** and verify that the supplier list and settings display correctly.
