# Managing Warehouse(s)

From osCommerce Wiki

[Jump to navigation](#mw-head)
[Jump to search](#searchInput)

This manual explains how to manage warehouses and warehouse location blocks in the admin area.

For a quick overview of this functionality, watch the video first.

The manual below explains everything step by step.

#### **1) Accessing Warehouse Management**

1. Go to the **admin area** of your website.
2. Click **Products/Catalogue → Warehouse(s)**.
3. Here you can:
   - **Filter** warehouses by **name** or **sales channel**
   - **Add** a new warehouse
   - **Manage location blocks**

[![Image 936.png](/wiki/images/thumb/d/dd/Image_936.png/799px-Image_936.png)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Image_936.png)

**2)** **Managing Location Blocks**

1. Click on **Manage location blocks**.
2. You can:
   - **Add** a new location block
   - **Edit** the name of an existing block
   - **Delete** a block by selecting it and clicking the delete option
3. Save your changes when finished.

   [![Image 937.png](/wiki/images/thumb/2/24/Image_937.png/799px-Image_937.png)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Image_937.png)

**3) Adding or Editing a Warehouse**

1. Click on **Add warehouse** to create a new one, or **select an existing warehouse** and click **Edit**.
2. While adding/editing a warehouse, you can configure:
   - **Warehouse Owner’s Name**
   - **Warehouse Name**
   - **Is Store** (Yes/No)
   - **Mark as Default Warehouse**
   - **Assign to Sales Channels**
   - **Email Address**
   - **Phone and Landline Number**
   - **Company Information** (Company Name, Tax/VAT Number, Company Number, Address)
   - **Additional Shipping Charge** (if applicable)
3. Click **Save** to apply changes.

[![Image 938.png](/wiki/images/thumb/9/9d/Image_938.png/799px-Image_938.png)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Image_938.png)

**4) Organizing Warehouse Locations**

1. Click on **Warehouse locations**.
2. You can **drag and drop** blocks to rearrange their layout.
3. Fill in the **required location details** within each block as needed.

[![Image 939.png](/wiki/images/thumb/6/6e/Image_939.png/799px-Image_939.png)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Image_939.png)
