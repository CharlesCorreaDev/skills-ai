# Managing Virtual Gift Card

From osCommerce Wiki

[Jump to navigation](#mw-head)
[Jump to search](#searchInput)

This manual explains how to create, configure, and offer virtual gift cards for customers to purchase and send.

For a quick overview of this functionality, watch the video first.

For detailed instructions, please refer to the manual below.

**1)** **Access the Admin Area**

1. Log in to the **Admin Area** of your website.
2. Navigate to **My Marketing Tools → Coupons → Virtual Gift Card**.

**2) Create Gift Card Amounts**

1. Under the **Virtual Gift Card** tab, create a **new gift card amount**.
2. Enter the **gift card value**.
3. Set the **gift card price** and **discounted price**.
4. Select the **sales channel** (if applicable).
5. Choose the **currency** for this gift card.
6. Save your changes.

[![Image 940.png](/wiki/images/thumb/1/11/Image_940.png/799px-Image_940.png)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Image_940.png)

**3)** **Customer Experience on the Front End**

1. Once configured, the gift card is available for purchase at:

**yourwebsite/catalog/gift-card**

2. Customers can:

- Select a **gift card design**.
- Choose a **gift card amount** from the dropdown.
- Write a **personal message**.
- Enter the **recipient’s name** and **email address**.
- Enter the **sender’s name**.

- Select a **date** for the gift card to be delivered to the recipient.
