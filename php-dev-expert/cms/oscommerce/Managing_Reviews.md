# Managing Reviews

From osCommerce Wiki

[Jump to navigation](#mw-head)
[Jump to search](#searchInput)

This manual explains how to manage customer reviews in your store using the admin area.

Start with the video for a quick introduction to this functionality.

The manual below provides step-by-step instructions.

**1) Accessing Reviews**

1. Log in to the admin area of your website.
2. Navigate to **Products/Catalogue** → **Reviews**.

**2) Filtering and Searching**

You can filter and search reviews by:

- Status (New, Approved, Declined)
- Customer ID
- Customer Name
- Product
- Date

**3) Batch Processing Reviews**

1. Select one or multiple reviews.
2. Choose the required action:
   - Approve
   - Decline
   - Delete

**4) Editing or Deleting Individual Reviews**

1. Click on a review to open it.
2. Use the available options:
   - **Approve/Decline**: switch the toggle after initial approval.
   - **Edit**: update the review text or rating (See the second screenshot).
   - **Delete**: remove the review.
