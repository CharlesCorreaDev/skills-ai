# Creating Manual Orders

From osCommerce Wiki

[Jump to navigation](#mw-head)
[Jump to search](#searchInput)

This manual explains how to create orders manually using customer and product details received offline.

For a quick overview of this functionality, watch the video first.

**The manual below explains everything step by step.**

**1) Accessing Manual Order Creation**

1.1. Log in to the admin area of your website.

1.2. Navigate to **Orders/Customers → Orders**.

1.3. Click on the **Create new order** button.

[![Image 893.png](/wiki/images/thumb/d/df/Image_893.png/799px-Image_893.png)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Image_893.png)

1.4. In the popup window, specify the **platform details** and confirm.

[![Image 892.png](/wiki/images/thumb/1/1a/Image_892.png/799px-Image_892.png)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Image_892.png)

**2)** **Adding Customer and Order Details**

2.1. Locate the **Customer Details** section.

2.2. Choose an existing customer by clicking **Assign customer**, or click **Add new customer**, and fill in the fields to create a new one.

2.3. Select the **payment method** and **shipping method** for the order.

[![Customer details sec.jpg](/wiki/images/thumb/a/ac/Customer_details_sec.jpg/801px-Customer_details_sec.jpg)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Customer_details_sec.jpg)

[![Add new customer.jpg](/wiki/images/thumb/8/88/Add_new_customer.jpg/800px-Add_new_customer.jpg)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Add_new_customer.jpg)

[![Shipping and payment.jpg](/wiki/images/thumb/b/b0/Shipping_and_payment.jpg/800px-Shipping_and_payment.jpg)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Shipping_and_payment.jpg)

**3) Adding Products to the Order**

3.1. Locate the **Products** section.

3.2. Click on **Add new product**.

[![Products sec.jpg](/wiki/images/thumb/4/42/Products_sec.jpg/800px-Products_sec.jpg)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Products_sec.jpg)

3.3. Search for products either:

- By browsing categories for the selected sales channel, or
- By typing the product name in the search field.

3.4. After adding products, you can:

- Add tax (if necessary)
- Change product quantity

[![Image 902.png](/wiki/images/thumb/0/05/Image_902.png/799px-Image_902.png)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Image_902.png)

- Update product price (in currency or %)
- Change product quantity again
- Remove products
- Return to product settings if needed

**4) Entering Payment Details**

4.1. Click on the **pencil icon** next to **Amount Paid** to enter the amount already paid by the customer.

[![Image 905.png](/wiki/images/thumb/8/83/Image_905.png/799px-Image_905.png)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Image_905.png)

**5)** **Completing the Order**

5.1. Click on the **Update and pay** button to finalize the order.

5.2. If the **Update and Pay** extension is installed, the option **Send customer request for payment** will appear in the confirmation popup.

[![Image 906.png](/wiki/images/thumb/c/c5/Image_906.png/800px-Image_906.png)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Image_906.png)

**6) Adding Order Total Elements**

6.1. Click on **Add Total Element** to include:

- Discount coupons
- Gift wrap
- Low order fee
- Credit amount
- Shipping fee
- Payment fee
- Subtotal for taxation
- Tax

[![Image 907.png](/wiki/images/thumb/2/20/Image_907.png/799px-Image_907.png)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Image_907.png)
