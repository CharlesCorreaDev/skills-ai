# Managing Filters on Categories

From osCommerce Wiki

[Jump to navigation](#mw-head)
[Jump to search](#searchInput)

This manual explains how to configure and display product filters on category pages.

Begin with the video to get an introduction.

The manual below explains everything step by step.

#### **1) Access the Properties Section**

1. Log in to the **admin area** of your website.
2. Navigate to **Products/Catalogue → Properties**.

#### **2) Add or Select a Property Category**

1. Either **add a new category** by clicking the **Add** **Category** button, **or** double-click an existing category to open it.

[![Image 865.png](/wiki/images/thumb/2/2e/Image_865.png/800px-Image_865.png)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Image_865.png)

**3) Enable Filters for Properties**

1. To create a new property, click the **Add Property** button.
2. To use an existing property, ensure that the **Filter toggle** is switched **ON**.
   - Only properties with the filter option enabled can appear as filters later.

[![Image 864.png](/wiki/images/thumb/4/49/Image_864.png/800px-Image_864.png)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Image_864.png)

**4) Assign Properties to Products**

1. Navigate to **Products**, then open a product for editing.
2. Assign the required **properties** (with values) to that product.

[![Image 863.png](/wiki/images/thumb/8/87/Image_863.png/800px-Image_863.png)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Image_863.png)

**5) Enable Filters in the Category**

1. Navigate to **Products/Catalogue → Products (Brands/Categories)**.
2. Find and open the category where filters are needed.
3. Click on the pencil icon or the **Edit** button.
4. Switch ON the toggles next to the **required filters** under the **Filters** tab.
   - **Only filters corresponding to the properties assigned to products in this category will be displayed.**

5. Save your changes.

6. Check the category on the storefront to ensure filters are displayed correctly.

[![Filter status for category.jpg](/wiki/images/thumb/8/8e/Filter_status_for_category.jpg/801px-Filter_status_for_category.jpg)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Filter_status_for_category.jpg)

[![Image 866.png](/wiki/images/thumb/7/71/Image_866.png/800px-Image_866.png)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Image_866.png)

By following these steps, you can effectively enable and manage product filters, helping customers find the right products more quickly and easily.
