# Managing Give Away

From osCommerce Wiki

[Jump to navigation](#mw-head)
[Jump to search](#searchInput)

This manual explains how to enable and configure the **Give Away** functionality in your osCommerce store to create promotional offers such as “Buy and Get Free” or “Free Product for Order Total.”

Watch the video for a brief overview of this functionality.

The manual below provides step-by-step instructions.

**1)** **Enable Give Away for a Product**

1. Access the **admin area** of your website.
2. While **adding or editing a product**, click on the **Marketing → Give-away** tab.
3. Switch **Give Away** **ON**.
4. Specify the **Start Date** and **End Date** for the promotion.
   - If the **End Date** field is left empty, the giveaway will never expire.

#### **2) Configure Give Away Type**

You can choose between two types of giveaway conditions:

**a. Order Total**

1. Click the **Order Total** tab.
2. In the **Order Amount** field, specify the minimum order total required to qualify for a giveaway product.
3. In the **Get for Free** field, specify the number of giveaway products to be received when the condition is met.

[![](/wiki/images/thumb/8/8b/1296_Give_away_order_total.jpg/801px-1296_Give_away_order_total.jpg)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:1296_Give_away_order_total.jpg)

**b. Buy and Get Free**

1. Click the **Buy and Get Free** tab.
2. In the **Buy q-ty** field, specify how many products must be purchased.
3. In the **Get for Free** field, specify the number of giveaway products provided for this quantity.
4. To include or exclude free products in the quantity discount, check or uncheck the box next to **Use free products in quantity discount**.
5. Use the **Add** **more** button to create additional giveaway conditions or the **trash icon** to delete unnecessary ones.

[![](/wiki/images/thumb/7/7c/1297_Buy_and_get_free.jpg/800px-1297_Buy_and_get_free.jpg)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:1297_Buy_and_get_free.jpg)

**3) Manage Giveaways via Admin Panel**

1. Navigate to **My Marketing Tools → Give Away** tabs.
2. Here you can:
   - **Add a new giveaway**
   - **Filter**, **edit**, or **delete** existing giveaways

[![1298 My Marketing tools.jpg](/wiki/images/thumb/3/3d/1298_My_Marketing_tools.jpg/800px-1298_My_Marketing_tools.jpg)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:1298_My_Marketing_tools.jpg)

**4) Add a New Giveaway**

1. Click the **Add New** button.
2. Select the required **product** from the list.

[![1299 Choose product.jpg](/wiki/images/thumb/0/02/1299_Choose_product.jpg/801px-1299_Choose_product.jpg)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:1299_Choose_product.jpg)

3. Configure all remaining giveaway settings as described above.

- **Note**: When adding a giveaway from this section, there is **an option to select a customer group** (e.g. Main, Advanced, or Basic).

[![1300 Set give away product.jpg](/wiki/images/thumb/b/b5/1300_Set_give_away_product.jpg/800px-1300_Set_give_away_product.jpg)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:1300_Set_give_away_product.jpg)

[![1301 New give away product.jpg](/wiki/images/thumb/a/a2/1301_New_give_away_product.jpg/801px-1301_New_give_away_product.jpg)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:1301_New_give_away_product.jpg)

The **Give Away** functionality is now configured.

Customers can automatically receive free products based on your selected rules - either by total order value or by purchasing a specific quantity.
