# Managing Customer Groups

From osCommerce Wiki

[Jump to navigation](#mw-head)
[Jump to search](#searchInput)

This manual explains how to manage customer groups in the admin area of your website.

Watch the video for a brief overview.

The manual below explains everything step by step.

**1) Accessing Customer Groups**

1.1. Log in to the admin area of your website.

1.2. Navigate to **Orders/Customers → Customer groups**.

**2) Creating and Managing Customer Groups**

2.1. To create a new customer group, click on the **Insert** **customer group** button.

2.2. To manage an existing customer group, click on the group in the list.

2.3. You can then use the following options:

- **Edit** – modify group settings
- **Edit Customers** – manage customers assigned to this group
- **Delete** – remove this customer group

[![](/wiki/images/thumb/c/c5/Cust_groups_sett1.jpg/801px-Cust_groups_sett1.jpg)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Cust_groups_sett1.jpg)

**3) Assigning Products and Categories to Customer Groups**

3.1. You can assign categories and products that will be visible only to this group.

1. Select the group in the list.
2. Click on the **Assign categories/products for show**.
3. In the popup, check the boxes to select categories and products.
4. Click **Assign**.

[![Cust groups sett2.jpg](/wiki/images/thumb/c/cd/Cust_groups_sett2.jpg/800px-Cust_groups_sett2.jpg)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Cust_groups_sett2.jpg)

**4) Editing Customer Group Settings**

4.1. Click **Edit** next to the required customer group.

4.2. Available settings include:

- **Is Tax Applicable?** – enable to apply tax (VAT) to this group
- **Accumulative discounts** – enable to set discounts based on the total amount spent

**Example of accumulative discount setup:**

- Minimum amount spent: **1000** → discount **1%**
- Minimum amount spent: **2000** → discount **2%** …and so on.

[![](/wiki/images/thumb/4/4d/1291_Customer_groups_edit.jpg/800px-1291_Customer_groups_edit.jpg)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:1291_Customer_groups_edit.jpg)

**5) Setting Default Customer Groups**

5.1. To make a group the **default** for new or guest users:

- Navigate to **Settings → Configuration → Customer Details**.
- Click on the required option and then click **Edit**.

[![](/wiki/images/thumb/6/6a/1292_Default_user_group_edit.jpg/800px-1292_Default_user_group_edit.jpg)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:1292_Default_user_group_edit.jpg)

- Select the required customer group from the drop-down list.

5.2. This group will now apply either to:

- **Non-logged (guest) users**, or
- **Newly registered customers**, depending on your selection.

[![](/wiki/images/thumb/a/ac/1293_Default_user_group_Basic.jpg/801px-1293_Default_user_group_Basic.jpg)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:1293_Default_user_group_Basic.jpg)
