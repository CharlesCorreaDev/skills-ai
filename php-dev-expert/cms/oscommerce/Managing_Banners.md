# Managing Banners

From osCommerce Wiki

[Jump to navigation](#mw-head)
[Jump to search](#searchInput)

This manual explains how to manage banners in the Banner Manager section of your website.

#### **1) Access the Banner Manager**

1. Log in to the **Admin Area** of your website.
2. Navigate to **My Marketing tools → Banner manager**.

#### **2) Work with Existing Banners**

1. To manage banners, use the table that lists all existing banners.
2. You can **add**, **edit**, **copy**, or **delete** banners by clicking the corresponding buttons.

[![1059.jpg](/wiki/images/thumb/5/54/1059.jpg/800px-1059.jpg)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:1059.jpg)

3. You can search banners by:

- **Sales channel**
- **Banner group**

[![1060 Search by.jpg](/wiki/images/thumb/a/a5/1060_Search_by.jpg/800px-1060_Search_by.jpg)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:1060_Search_by.jpg)

The banners table displays:

- **Banners** – banner name
- **Groups** – group to which the banner belongs
- **Sales channel name** – where the banner is used
- **Status** – active/inactive (toggle)

Also, use the **quick search field** to locate banners quickly.

[![1061 Banners table.jpg](/wiki/images/thumb/e/e1/1061_Banners_table.jpg/800px-1061_Banners_table.jpg)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:1061_Banners_table.jpg)

#### **3) Add or Edit a Banner**

1. Click **New Banner**, or click **Edit** next to an existing banner. (see the first screenshot)

#### **4) Link a Banner to Sales Channels**

1. Open the **Sales Channels** tab.
2. Select which **sales channels** the banner should be displayed on.

[![1062 Sales channel tab.jpg](/wiki/images/thumb/6/69/1062_Sales_channel_tab.jpg/800px-1062_Sales_channel_tab.jpg)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:1062_Sales_channel_tab.jpg)

#### **5) Configure Main Details**

1. Open the **Main details** tab.
2. Set:
   - **Banner group**
   - **Sort order**
   - **Start date (Scheduled At)**
   - **End date (Expires On)**
   - **nofollow** link attribute (optional, for SEO purposes)

[![1063 Main details tab.jpg](/wiki/images/thumb/6/62/1063_Main_details_tab.jpg/800px-1063_Main_details_tab.jpg)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:1063_Main_details_tab.jpg)

#### **6) Configure Name and Description**

1. Open the **Name and Description** tab.
2. Set:
   - **Banner title**
   - **Banner URL** (enter manually or choose from pages, products, categories, brands, or common links)
   - **Upload an image**, if applicable
   - **Optional HTML text content**

[![1064 Name and descr tab 1.jpg](/wiki/images/thumb/2/28/1064_Name_and_descr_tab_1.jpg/800px-1064_Name_and_descr_tab_1.jpg)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:1064_Name_and_descr_tab_1.jpg)

#### **7) Choose a Banner Type**

You can choose one of the following banner types:

- **Image**
- **HTML text**
- **Text and Image** (place text over the image)

[![1065 Name and descr tab 2.jpg](/wiki/images/thumb/2/2b/1065_Name_and_descr_tab_2.jpg/800px-1065_Name_and_descr_tab_2.jpg)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:1065_Name_and_descr_tab_2.jpg)

- **SVG** (use the built-in **SVG editor**)

[![1066 Name and descr tab 3 SVG.jpg](/wiki/images/thumb/d/d3/1066_Name_and_descr_tab_3_SVG.jpg/800px-1066_Name_and_descr_tab_3_SVG.jpg)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:1066_Name_and_descr_tab_3_SVG.jpg)

- **Video** (upload a video file)

[![1067 Name and descr tab 4 Video.jpg](/wiki/images/thumb/3/39/1067_Name_and_descr_tab_4_Video.jpg/800px-1067_Name_and_descr_tab_4_Video.jpg)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:1067_Name_and_descr_tab_4_Video.jpg)
