# Assigning and Moving Products to Categories

From osCommerce Wiki

[Jump to navigation](#mw-head)
[Jump to search](#searchInput)

This manual explains how to assign products to categories and how to move them between categories.

For a quick overview of this functionality, watch the video first.

**For detailed guidance, please refer to the manual below.**

#### **1) Access Categories**

1. Log in to the **admin area** of your website.
2. Go to **Products/Catalogue**.
3. Select the required category under the **Categories** tab, or use the **filter** to find it.

#### **2) Assign a New Product to a Category**

1. After selecting a category, click the **Add product** button.
2. Create the product as usual — it will be automatically assigned to the chosen category.

[![](/wiki/images/thumb/3/3b/1274_Choosing_categories.jpg/800px-1274_Choosing_categories.jpg)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:1274_Choosing_categories.jpg)

[![1275 Add product.jpg](/wiki/images/thumb/9/9c/1275_Add_product.jpg/800px-1275_Add_product.jpg)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:1275_Add_product.jpg)

#### **3) Move an Existing Product to Another Category**

1. Click on the required product.
2. Click the **Move** button.

[![](/wiki/images/thumb/1/1e/1276_Move_product.jpg/801px-1276_Move_product.jpg)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:1276_Move_product.jpg)

3. Select the required category from the **drop-down list**.

4. Click **Move** to confirm.

[![](/wiki/images/thumb/7/79/1277_Select_new_category_for_a_product.jpg/801px-1277_Select_new_category_for_a_product.jpg)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:1277_Select_new_category_for_a_product.jpg)

#### **4) Assign an Existing Product to Multiple Categories**

1. Click on the required product.
2. Click the **Copy To** button.

[![](/wiki/images/thumb/f/fa/1278_Copy_to.jpg/801px-1278_Copy_to.jpg)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:1278_Copy_to.jpg)

3. Select the additional category from the **drop-down list**.

4. Under **Copy Method**, select **Link product**.

5. Click **Copy**.

*This allows the product to appear in several categories without duplication.*

[![](/wiki/images/thumb/9/9c/1279_Copy_to_link_product.jpg/801px-1279_Copy_to_link_product.jpg)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:1279_Copy_to_link_product.jpg)

#### **5) Move or Assign Products in Batch**

1. In the product list, check the boxes next to multiple products (or use **Select all** - the top box).
2. When batch action buttons appear, click **Move**.

[![](/wiki/images/thumb/e/e8/1280_Batch_actions.jpg/800px-1280_Batch_actions.jpg)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:1280_Batch_actions.jpg)

3. In the pop-up window, choose whether to:

- **Move** products to another category, or
- **Link** products to additional categories.

4. Confirm your selection.

[![](/wiki/images/thumb/6/66/1281_Pop_up_window.jpg/800px-1281_Pop_up_window.jpg)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:1281_Pop_up_window.jpg)

#### **6) Assign Products to Multiple Categories via Import/Export**

1. Go to **Products/Catalogue → Import/Export**.
2. Open the **Manual export** tab.
3. Select **Products** from the drop-down list.
4. Select the required category and click **Download**.
5. In the pop-up, select how many category columns you want to work with (up to 6).

[![](/wiki/images/thumb/2/2a/1282_6_Categories.jpg/800px-1282_6_Categories.jpg)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:1282_6_Categories.jpg)

6. In the downloaded **CSV file**, assign products to the required categories following the sample structure.

[![Image 1098.png](/wiki/images/thumb/d/da/Image_1098.png/800px-Image_1098.png)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:Image_1098.png)

7. Return to **Manual import**, click **Browse**, select your file, and then click **Upload**.

8. When the file appears in the list, click the **Play** icon ▶ to apply changes.

[![](/wiki/images/thumb/c/c6/1283_Play_icon.jpg/800px-1283_Play_icon.jpg)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:1283_Play_icon.jpg)

#### **7) Assign Products to Unlimited Categories**

- Instead of the standard product export, select **Assign products to categories** under Import/Export.
- Proceed with export → update → upload and run changes in the same way as described above.

[![](/wiki/images/thumb/a/ab/1284_Assign_prod_to_categ.jpg/801px-1284_Assign_prod_to_categ.jpg)](https://www.oscommerce.com/wiki/File:1284_Assign_prod_to_categ.jpg)
