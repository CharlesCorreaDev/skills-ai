## Support Options[â](#support-options "Direct link to Support Options")

### Phone[â](#phone "Direct link to Phone")

Phone support is provided to owned license holders with current Support and Updates, as well as monthly license holders with current accounts. Before calling support, please have your license key ready, which can be obtained from your account.

### Support Tickets[â](#support-tickets "Direct link to Support Tickets")

Ticket support is provided to owned license holders with current Support and Updates, as well as monthly license holders with current accounts.To ensure the quick response time, please [log into your account](https://account.blesta.com/client/login/) before [opening a ticket](https://account.blesta.com/client/plugin/support_manager/client_tickets/departments/).

Community support is available and free to everyone, simply visit the [Community Support Forum](http://www.blesta.com/forums/index.php?/forum/23-support/).
