Blesta is a powerful, security-focused client management, billing, and support platform designed for both users and developers. Built in PHP on our open-source minPHP MVC framework, Blesta is modular, object-oriented, and highly extensible.

Originally developed in 2003 for Phillips Data, Inc., our parent company, Blesta addressed the need for a robust billing solution when no suitable alternatives existed. Publicly launched in 2007 with version 1, Blesta gained widespread popularity with version 2 for its remarkable simplicity. Now, with version 3 and beyond, Blesta has been completely reengineered to blend intuitive design with a seamless front-end and back-end experience. Our commitment to excellence ensures a no-compromise approach to delivering a reliable, user-friendly platform.
