[## ðï¸ Encryption

Encryption is a means of modifying plain-text into an unreadable format. Algorithms used to encrypt text are known as ciphers. For example, replacing each letter in a message with another letter in the alphabet is a simple form of encryption known as a Caesar Cipher. Today's algorithms are much more advanced, though many systems still rely on proprietary ciphers that are no more advanced than a simple Caesar Cipher. Blesta, however, only uses properly vetted, and verifiably secure, open encryption ciphers.](docs__5__security__encryption.md)
