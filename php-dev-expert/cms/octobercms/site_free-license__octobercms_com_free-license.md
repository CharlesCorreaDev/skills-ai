# October CMS Free License

## All Features Unlocked, No Strings Attached

Every October CMS account includes a complimentary license. This license enables you to build one project. This license comes with all features and no restrictions, making it ideal for any project, whether personal or commercial.

Functioning just like our paid licenses, the Free License provides one year of software updates. Once the license expires, you can get another year of updates for just $39. Extending the license is only necessary if you wish to update the platform or plugins. You are legally permitted to continue running a website indefinitely after the license expires.

[#### Obtaining October CMS Free License

Learn how to obtain a free October CMS license and use it to install the platform.

Watch the tutorial](https://youtu.be/iyW5qPw8cGk)

## Obtaining the Free License

The instructions below explain how to obtain the free license. Alternatively, you can watch the [video tutorial](https://youtu.be/iyW5qPw8cGk).

- [Create an October CMS account](/account/register) or log into your existing one.
- Navigate to the [Account / Projects](/account/project/dashboard) page and create a project. You may also use an existing project.
- Click on "Get a Free License" under the **License tab**.
- For installation instructions, please refer to the [documentation](https://docs.octobercms.com).
