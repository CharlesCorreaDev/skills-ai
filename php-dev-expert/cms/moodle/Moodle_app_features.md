  #### See your courses at glance

  Your courses are listed with easy access to contents, participants, grades and notes. A useful filter field lets you find a particular course quickly. If your token expires during a session, you're prompted to re-enter your password and continue.
- [![](/503/en/images_en/thumb/7/71/mobile35timeline.png/600px-mobile35timeline.png)](https://docs.moodle.org/503/en/File:mobile35timeline.png)

  #### View and access activities which are due

  The timeline display shows you which tasks you need to complete, with quick access links.

- [![](/503/en/images_en/thumb/8/8c/mobile35connectparticipants1.png/600px-mobile35connectparticipants1.png)](https://docs.moodle.org/503/en/File:mobile35connectparticipants1.png)

  #### Connect with course participants

  Easily connect to course participants.
- [![](/503/en/images_en/thumb/b/bb/mobile35dashboardbothorientations.png/600px-mobile35dashboardbothorientations.png)](https://docs.moodle.org/503/en/File:mobile35dashboardbothorientations.png)

  #### Orientation and resolution support

  The app displays in portrait or landscape view, whatever your screen resolution.

- [![](/503/en/images_en/thumb/c/cb/mobile35accesscourses.png/600px-mobile35accesscourses.png)](https://docs.moodle.org/503/en/File:mobile35accesscourses.png)

  #### Easily access course content

  View course activities and download materials for offline use. Work through Books and IMS Content packages directly in the app. The downloading status of resources is preserved even when changing sections or pages.
- [![](/503/en/images_en/thumb/1/1c/mobile35activitycompletion.png/600px-mobile35activitycompletion.png)](https://docs.moodle.org/503/en/File:mobile35activitycompletion.png)

  #### Activity completion

  Track progress from your device with [Activity completion](Activity_completion.md "Activity completion"). Automatic completion is registered, and students can also manually mark a task complete on their mobile.

- [![](/503/en/images_en/thumb/4/46/mobile35gradebook.png/600px-mobile35gradebook.png)](https://docs.moodle.org/503/en/File:mobile35gradebook.png)

  #### Grades and grading

  A Grades link for each course gives fingertip access to the gradebook, and teachers can view assignment submissions on the move.
- [![](/503/en/images_en/thumb/1/11/mobile35notes.png/600px-mobile35notes.png)](https://docs.moodle.org/503/en/File:mobile35notes.png)

  #### Notes

  Teachers can view site, course and personal notes about their students and add their own notes.

- [![](/503/en/images_en/thumb/5/54/mobile35messages.png/600px-mobile35messages.png)](https://docs.moodle.org/503/en/File:mobile35messages.png)

  #### Message participants

  Send and view private messages to colleagues and students from the Messages link in the side tab.
- [![](/503/en/images_en/thumb/3/36/mobile35calendar.png/600px-mobile35calendar.png)](https://docs.moodle.org/503/en/File:mobile35calendar.png)

  #### Keep up to date with calendar events

  Site, course and user events can be viewed in the calendar.Calendar events can now be viewed offline and infinite scrolling is available.

- [![](/503/en/images_en/thumb/f/f8/mobile35privatefiles.png/600px-mobile35privatefiles.png)](https://docs.moodle.org/503/en/File:mobile35privatefiles.png)

  #### View and upload to your Private files

  Check the contents of your private files and upload media to them on the go. You're now allowed up to 50MB storage (depending on your SD card).
- [![](/503/en/images_en/thumb/2/26/mobile35notifications.png/600px-mobile35notifications.png)](https://docs.moodle.org/503/en/File:mobile35notifications.png)

  #### Notifications

  Keep up to date with notifications. The app supports both local (calendar events) and push (messages, forum posts, submitted assignments etc) notifications. See [Mobile app notifications](Mobile_app_notifications.md "Mobile app notifications") for more details. Infinite scrolling is available and local notifications have multi-site support.

- [![](/503/en/images_en/thumb/2/25/mobile35choice.png/600px-mobile35choice.png)](https://docs.moodle.org/503/en/File:mobile35choice.png)

  #### Make choices

  The app supports students making and removing choices.
- [![](/503/en/images_en/thumb/8/86/mobile35searchcourses.png/600px-mobile35searchcourses.png)](https://docs.moodle.org/503/en/File:mobile35searchcourses.png)

  #### Search the list of courses

  App users can search courses to find one they wish to take.

- [![](/503/en/images_en/thumb/0/00/mobile35selfenrol.png/600px-mobile35selfenrol.png)](https://docs.moodle.org/503/en/File:mobile35selfenrol.png)

  #### Enrol yourself from your smartphone

  Found a course you like? Enrol immediately via the app.
- [![](/503/en/images_en/thumb/7/71/mobile35scorm.png/600px-mobile35scorm.png)](https://docs.moodle.org/503/en/File:mobile35scorm.png)

  #### Play SCORM packages on and off-line

  The [Moodle app SCORM player](Moodle_app_SCORM_player.md "Moodle app SCORM player") tracks interactions and synchronises when you're back online.

- [![](/503/en/images_en/thumb/b/b8/mobile35downloadcourse.png/600px-mobile35downloadcourse.png)](https://docs.moodle.org/503/en/File:mobile35downloadcourse.png)

  #### Download all course sections

  Easily download all sections to view offline.
- [![](/503/en/images_en/thumb/7/7c/mobile35glossary.png/600px-mobile35glossary.png)](https://docs.moodle.org/503/en/File:mobile35glossary.png)

  #### Browse glossary entries

  Search for and browse glossary entries.

- [![](/503/en/images_en/thumb/d/d0/mobile35quiz.png/600px-mobile35quiz.png)](https://docs.moodle.org/503/en/File:mobile35quiz.png)

  #### Take quizzes on your mobile

  Quizzes may now be completed on the move. Find out more about the [Moodle Mobile quiz](Moodle_Mobile_Quiz_module.md "Moodle Mobile Quiz module").
- [![](/503/en/images_en/thumb/9/9c/mobile35learningplans.png/600px-mobile35learningplans.png)](https://docs.moodle.org/503/en/File:mobile35learningplans.png)

  #### Learning plans

  Learning plans may be viewed and progress checked from the mobile.

- [![](/503/en/images_en/thumb/9/96/mobile35competencies.png/600px-mobile35competencies.png)](https://docs.moodle.org/503/en/File:mobile35competencies.png)

  #### Easiy view course competencies

  A Competencies tab allows you to see course competencies at a glance.
- [![](/503/en/images_en/thumb/1/1e/mobile35teachergrading.png/600px-mobile35teachergrading.png)](https://docs.moodle.org/503/en/File:mobile35teachergrading.png)

  #### Teachers: Grade assignments

  Assignments may be graded on the move, from the app and may be graded offline

- [![](/503/en/images_en/thumb/a/a7/mobile35badges.png/600px-mobile35badges.png)](https://docs.moodle.org/503/en/File:mobile35badges.png)

  #### View badges from your profile

  Badges are now visible from a link in the user profile
- [![](/503/en/images_en/thumb/5/5b/mobile35preferences.png/600px-mobile35preferences.png)](https://docs.moodle.org/503/en/File:mobile35preferences.png)

  #### Notifications & Messaging preferences

  New preferences screen lets you decide how to be alerted

- [![](/503/en/images_en/thumb/3/3f/mobile35workshop.png/600px-mobile35workshop.png)](https://docs.moodle.org/503/en/File:mobile35workshop.png)

  #### Submit to workshops from the app

  Students can now do workshop activities from their mobile or tablet.
- [![](/503/en/images_en/thumb/2/2c/mobile35paypal.png/600px-mobile35paypal.png)](https://docs.moodle.org/503/en/File:mobile35paypal.png)

  #### Sign up to courses via PayPal

  PayPal enrolment is now supported in the app.

- [![](/503/en/images_en/thumb/e/e3/mobile39h5p.png/600px-mobile39h5p.png)](https://docs.moodle.org/503/en/File:mobile39h5p.png)

  #### Play H5P on and off-line

  Users can now play [H5P packages](Moodle_app_H5P_support.md "Moodle app H5P support") on and off-line, including grading for the H5P activity.
- [![](/503/en/images_en/thumb/f/f5/mobile39qr.png/600px-mobile39qr.png)](https://docs.moodle.org/503/en/File:mobile39qr.png)

  #### Scan QRs to login or open courses/activities in the app

  The app now includes a QR reader so users can easily authenticate in their site or open certain courses or activities in the app.

(ar) عربي, (bg) Български, (ca) Català, (cs) Čeština, (da) Dansk, (de) Deutsch, (de-du) Deutsch - Du, (el) Ελληνικά, (en) English, (en-us) English - United States, (es) Español, (es-mx) Español - México, (eu) Euskara, (fa) فارسی, (fi) Suomi, (fr) Français, (he) עברית, (hu) magyar, (id) Indonesian, (it) Italiano, (ja) 日本語, (km) ខ្មែរ, (kn) ಕನ್ನಡ, (ko) 한국어, (lt) Lietuvių, (lv) Latviešu, (mn) मराठी, (mr) मराठी, (nl) Nederlands, (no) Norsk - bokmål, (pl) Polski, (pt-br) Português - Brasil, (pt) Português - Portugal, (pt-br) Português - Brasil, (ro) Română, (ru) Русский, (sl) Slovenščina, (sr-cr) Српски, (sr-lt) Srpski, (sv) Svenska, (tg) Тоҷикӣ, (tr) Türkçe, (uk) Українська, (vi) Vietnamese, (zh-cn) 简体中文, (zh-tw) 正體中文
