In Moodle, a teacher has responsibility for the materials in their own course. They often also manage enrolments and are able to change the layout of the course page. This quick start guide introduces Moodle's features to those with the [Teacher role](Teacher_role.md "Teacher role").

## Course set up

A course is a space on a Moodle site where teachers can add learning materials for their students. A teacher may have more than one course and a course may include more than one teacher and more than one group of learners. See the screencast [What is a course?](https://youtu.be/UbZoN17hrmM?si=yIlIl5W4DvxILpKj) .

By default a regular teacher can't add a new course. See [Adding a new course](Adding_a_new_course.md "Adding a new course") for information on how courses may be created.

[![](/503/en/images_en/thumb/1/17/Boost40.png/600px-Boost40.png)](https://docs.moodle.org/503/en/File:Boost40.png)

Example of a Moodle course using the [Boost theme](Boost_theme.md "Boost theme")

[![](/503/en/images_en/thumb/9/9d/Classic40.png/600px-Classic40.png)](https://docs.moodle.org/503/en/File:Classic40.png)

Example of a Moodle course using the [Classic theme](Classic_theme.md "Classic theme")

A course can display its materials in a number of ways or 'formats', for example in weekly sections or named topic sections. You can show all the sections at once or just reveal one at a time. See [Course settings](Course_settings.md "Course settings") for more information, or watch the screencast [Course layout](https://youtu.be/X_QxG-qVcA4).

Course sections may be renamed by turning on the editing and clicking the configuration icon underneath the section name. Sections may also be moved by drag and drop and sections added or removed by clicking the + or - underneath the bottom section. See [Course homepage](Course_homepage.md "Course homepage") for more information.

You can also add elements, known as 'blocks', to the right, left or both sides of your central learning area, depending on your theme. For more information see [Blocks](Blocks.md "Blocks").

## Course enrolment

Before a learner can access your course, they must first be  [authenticated on the site](Authentication.md "Authentication") and that is the responsibility of the site administrator.

[![](/503/en/images_en/thumb/7/71/Enrol40.png/600px-Enrol40.png)](https://docs.moodle.org/503/en/File:Enrol40.png)

Enrolment methods

There are several [enrolment methods](Enrolment_methods.md "Enrolment methods") available to a teacher, what is available, again, depends on the site administrator. The admin might enrol students into course automatically, or the teacher might be able to let students enrol themselves. This is the [Manual enrolment](Manual_enrolment.md "Manual enrolment") method. Other options can be seen in the **Enrolment methods** link and include [Self enrolment](Self_enrolment.md "Self enrolment") and [Guest access](Guest_access.md "Guest access").

**Note:** Guests can only view the course and its resources; they cannot participate in any activities.

An enrolment key may be set if [Self enrolment](Self_enrolment.md "Self enrolment") is enabled so that only students with the key can enter. The screencast [Giving learners course access](https://youtu.be/rlfiLRQhJRw?si=GIMFuL4Eh4D5ZpBo) gives more information on course enrolment.

## Grading

Every course has its own [Gradebook](Gradebook.md "Gradebook") which can record scores from [assignments](Assignment.md "Assignment") , [quizzes](Quiz.md "Quiz") , [peer assessment workshops](Workshop.md "Workshop"), ['branching' lessons](Lesson.md "Lesson"), [SCORM activities](SCORM_activity.md "SCORM activity") and [LTI learning resources](External_tool.md "External tool").

[![](/503/en/images_en/thumb/9/9a/grades40.png/600px-grades40.png)](https://docs.moodle.org/503/en/File:grades40.png)

The grader report

Other activities such as [forums](Forum.md "Forum"), [databases](Database.md "Database") and [glossaries](Glossary.md "Glossary") can have ratings enabled which will also then be reflected in the gradebook.

Grade items can be manually created from *Course navigation > Grades > Gradebook setup* , clicking Add on the right and selecting *Add grade item*. and It is also possible to [import](Grade_import.md "Grade import") and [export](Grade_export.md "Grade export") grades.

## Tracking progress

If [completion tracking](Activity_completion.md "Activity completion") has been enabled for the site and in *Course navigation > Settings*, you can then set completion conditions in activity settings. A button with completion requirement information will appear to the right of the activity and a student may either be allowed to check it manually, or the item will show complete once the criteria for that particular activity have been met. Completion indicators (circles) display in the course index and reflect the completion status of each activity, for quick checking.

This feature can be combined with [Course completion](Course_completion.md "Course completion") so that when certain activities have been completed and/or grades obtained, the course itself is marked complete.

[Badges](Badges.md "Badges") can also be issued, manually or based on completion criteria to act as a motivator and record of course progress.

## Restricting access

A course can be set to display everything to everyone all the time, or it can be very restrictive, displaying certain items at certain times, to certain groups or based on performance in previous tasks.

[![](/503/en/images_en/8/84/restrict40.png)](https://docs.moodle.org/503/en/File:restrict40.png)

Options for restricting access

Course items and sections may be manually hidden using the 'hide' icon when the editing is turned on. If [Restrict access](Restrict_access.md "Restrict access") has been enabled by the administrator then a Restrict access section will display in each course activity and resource, allowing you to choose how and when this item will be revealed, and whether to hide it completely or show it greyed out with the conditions for access.
