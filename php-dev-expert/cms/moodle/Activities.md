An activity is a general name for a group of features in a Moodle course. Usually an activity is something that a student will do that interacts with other students and or the teacher.

In Moodle terminology, an Activity, such as Forums or Quizzes, properly means something students can contribute to directly, and is often contrasted to a [Resource](Resources.md "Resources") such as a File or Page, which is presented by the teacher to them. However, the term activity is sometimes for convenience also used to refer to both Activities and Resources as a group.

There are 15 different types of activities in the standard Moodle that can be found when the editing is turned on and the link 'Add an activity or resource' is clicked.

[Assignments](Assignment_activity.md "Assignment activity")
:   Enable teachers to grade and give comments on uploaded files and assignments created on and off line

[BigBlueButton](BigBlueButton.md "BigBlueButton")
:   Run live video conferencing sessions within Moodle

[Choice](Choice_activity.md "Choice activity")
:   A teacher asks a question and specifies a choice of multiple responses

[Database](Database_activity.md "Database activity")
:   Enables participants to create, maintain and search a bank of record entries

[Feedback](Feedback_activity.md "Feedback activity")
:   For creating and conducting surveys to collect feedback.

[Forum](Forum_activity.md "Forum activity")
:   Allows participants to have asynchronous discussions

[Glossary](Glossary_activity.md "Glossary activity")
:   Enables participants to create and maintain a list of definitions, like a dictionary

[H5P activity](H5P_activity.md "H5P activity")
:   Enables H5P content created in the Content bank or on h5p.com or with the [lumi App](https://lumi.education/) to be easily added to a course as an activity.

[Lesson](Lesson_activity.md "Lesson activity")
:   For delivering content in flexible ways

[LTI External tool activity](LTI_External_tool_activity.md "LTI External tool activity")
:   Allows participants to interact with LTI compliant learning resources and activities on other web sites.

[Quiz](Quiz_activity.md "Quiz activity")
:   Allows the teacher to design and set quiz tests, which may be automatically marked and feedback and/or to correct answers shown

[SCORM](SCORM_activity.md "SCORM activity")
:   Enables SCORM packages to be included as course content

[Wiki](Wiki_activity.md "Wiki activity")
:   A collection of web pages that anyone can add to or edit

[Workshop](Workshop_activity.md "Workshop activity")
:   Enables peer assessment

Additional plugins from the [Moodle plugins directory](http://moodle.org/plugins) may also be installed on your site.
