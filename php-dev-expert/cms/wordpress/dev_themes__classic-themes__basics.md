# Theme Basics

In this chapter, you’ll begin learning how to build a theme. The anatomy of a theme and its parts will be broken down and explained.

You’ll begin by understanding the concepts of building a theme by looking at [theme files](dev_themes__basics__template-files.md) and [post types](dev_themes__basics__post-types.md). Then you’ll learn how to keep your files [organized](dev_themes__basics__organizing-theme-files.md) within your theme.

You’ll also look at [The Loop](dev_themes__basics__the-loop.md "The Loop"), which is responsible for pulling content out of the WordPress database.

Finally, you’ll learn more about adding features to your theme by [using theme functions](dev_themes__basics__theme-functions.md "Theme Functions"), [including CSS & JavaScript](dev_themes__basics__including-css-javascript.md "Including CSS & JavaScript"), leveraging [conditional tags](dev_themes__basics__conditional-tags.md "Conditional Tags") to only show content you need, and working with [default taxonomies and creating your own](dev_themes__basics__categories-tags-custom-taxonomies.md).
