When creating or editing a WordPress page or blog post, you can easily add images to a post or page using the Image Block.

**Note:** For the most up-to-date and detailed instructions on how to add, edit, and customize images in the Block Editor, please refer to the official **[Image block guide](article__image-block.md)**.
