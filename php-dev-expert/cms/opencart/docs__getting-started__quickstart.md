## Introduction

This guide will help you quickly set up your OpenCart store after installation. Follow the step-by-step instructions below to get your store running in minutes.

1

#### 1. Access Your Admin Panel

Your admin URL is typically `yourdomain.com/admin` - use the credentials you created during installation.

- Navigate to your admin URL (usually `yourdomain.com/admin`)
- Log in with the credentials created during installation

![OpenCart Admin Login Screen](https://docs.opencart.com/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2F3646803-files.gitbook.io%2F%7E%2Ffiles%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252F4cP5wmQoIzTALooMO6m7%252Fuploads%252FrUNYO8cXu4vr5vCL3nGR%252Fadmin-login.png%3Falt%3Dmedia%26token%3Dfe28e383-f87d-437f-a753-775e5a367a95&width=768&dpr=3&quality=100&sign=3da39975cf388c6ef7bf670e01e22d91&sv=3)

Admin login screen - enter your credentials to access the dashboard

2

#### 2. Basic Store Configuration

Configure your store's basic information before adding products. This ensures customers see accurate store details.

- Go to **System > Settings**
- Configure your store name, email, and contact information
- Set up your default currency and language

![Store Configuration Settings](https://docs.opencart.com/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2F3646803-files.gitbook.io%2F%7E%2Ffiles%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252F4cP5wmQoIzTALooMO6m7%252Fuploads%252FpKLxzPhCg55bsg2VBSzF%252Fadmin-configure-store.png%3Falt%3Dmedia%26token%3D5975f223-42b9-4ea6-9974-d2f13e4dd92a&width=768&dpr=3&quality=100&sign=4646963c4b1f116d9246b1830dcd01d8&sv=3)

Store settings page - configure your store name, contact info, and regional settings

3

#### 3. Add Your First Product

Start with a simple product to understand the process. You can add more complex products with options and attributes later.

- Go to **Catalog > Products**
- Click "Add New"
- Fill in product details, pricing, and images
- Save and publish

![Add New Product Interface](https://docs.opencart.com/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2F3646803-files.gitbook.io%2F%7E%2Ffiles%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252F4cP5wmQoIzTALooMO6m7%252Fuploads%252FF9kzpDJyx5fl9gOROSyI%252Fadmin-add-product.png%3Falt%3Dmedia%26token%3Dd03d96d8-d004-4577-a8a0-7c8311205cd1&width=768&dpr=3&quality=100&sign=58e6f5ec5a59cafdfa1914d7658b1cd1&sv=3)

Product creation form - add product details, pricing, and upload images

4

#### 4. Set Up Payment Methods

**Important**: Configure at least one payment method before testing your store. Without payment methods, customers cannot complete purchases.

- Go to **Extensions > Payments**
- Install and configure your preferred payment gateways

![Payment Methods Configuration](https://docs.opencart.com/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2F3646803-files.gitbook.io%2F%7E%2Ffiles%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252F4cP5wmQoIzTALooMO6m7%252Fuploads%252FZmaeY1uBXddxF0vtEvY4%252Fadmin-payments.png%3Falt%3Dmedia%26token%3Dc19e1ed2-2756-482f-8bfa-1af9b7002b02&width=768&dpr=3&quality=100&sign=e61ae3dc5fa9adedb7f0025bfe283c2f&sv=3)

Payment methods page - install and configure payment gateways like PayPal, Stripe, or bank transfer

5

#### 5. Configure Shipping

Set up shipping methods that match your business model. Common options include flat rate, weight-based, or free shipping.

- Go to **Extensions > Shipping**
- Set up shipping methods for your products

![Shipping Methods Configuration](https://docs.opencart.com/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2F3646803-files.gitbook.io%2F%7E%2Ffiles%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252F4cP5wmQoIzTALooMO6m7%252Fuploads%252Fi0Bx4mGwYlHHLvs0bqpV%252Fadmin-shipping.png%3Falt%3Dmedia%26token%3Dc67d90ad-e0a6-4394-ba4e-39694eb184b9&width=768&dpr=3&quality=100&sign=eb8bbeb0dc30c9817cffdfb3ce790b4f&sv=3)

Shipping methods page - configure shipping options based on your products and locations

6

#### 6. Test Your Store

**Congratulations!** Your store is now ready for testing. Make a test purchase to ensure everything works correctly.

- Visit your store frontend
- Test the shopping cart and checkout process
- Verify that payments and shipping calculations work correctly
- Add product to cart
- Proceed to checkout
- Complete payment process
- Verify order confirmation
- Check admin order management
