## Looking for a developer?

Go to [freelancer.com](https://www.freelancer.com/freelancers) and enter "freescout" into the search field and make sure to remove "Online Users" filter.

![2021-05-17_20-28-25](https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/40499291/118531386-76005f00-b74e-11eb-841b-bac13606db9d.png)

If you are looking specifically for help with FreeScout installation - see [this page](https://freescout.net/installation-services/).

## Want to be hired as a developer?

Register on [freelancer.com](https://www.freelancer.com/) and mention "FreeScout" in your profile.
